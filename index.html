A focused workflow

From a clip to a clean handoff.

Vevlo is built for one job: helping you create editable text and standard subtitle files from media you already have.

01Import

Choose a video from Photos or import a file.

02Transcribe

Create timed English text on your device where supported.

03Review

Edit each cue and jump to its place in the media.

04Export

Share TXT, SRT, or VTT with the next app or person.

Made for delivery

Less workspace. More finished files.

01

Review timed text

Correct the words before you send anything. Tap a cue time to review that moment in your media.

02

Export standard formats

Create UTF-8 TXT, numbered SRT, or WebVTT files that fit into the rest of your workflow.

03

Keep projects local

Your imported media and transcript projects stay in Vevlo’s local device storage until you delete them.

V1 supports MP4, MOV, MP3, M4A, and WAV. English transcription only. Review your text before publishing—recognition quality depends on the recording.

Private by design.
Simple by default.

Vevlo does not require an account. It does not upload your media files to Vevlo servers for transcription, and it does not include advertising, tracking, or analytics SDKs.
