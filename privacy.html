Privacy Policy

Privacy that matches the product.

Last updated: August 11, 2026. This policy applies when Vevlo is made available to users.

Summary

Vevlo is designed to turn supported English video and audio into editable text and subtitle files on your device. Vevlo does not require an account, does not upload your media files or transcript projects to Vevlo servers for transcription, and does not include advertising, tracking, or analytics SDKs.

1. Information stored on your device

When you choose media in Vevlo, the app stores the imported source file, project metadata, transcript text, and temporary files needed to prepare or export your work in Vevlo’s local device storage. Vevlo also stores a local count of completed free transcriptions so it can apply the free-use limit.

You can delete a project in Vevlo. Deleting a project removes that project’s imported media, transcript, and project files from Vevlo’s local storage. Temporary processing and export files are cleaned up as part of the app’s normal workflow.

2. Speech recognition and media processing

Vevlo requests permission to use Apple Speech Recognition for English transcription. Vevlo requests on-device speech-recognition capability where it is supported and does not use a Vevlo cloud service to transcribe your media. If on-device recognition is not available for a supported use case, Vevlo is designed to show an actionable message rather than silently switch to a Vevlo upload service.

Apple’s handling of its own operating system and speech services is governed by Apple’s policies. Vevlo does not promise that iCloud Photos or iCloud Drive will never download media that you have already stored with Apple; that download is separate from uploading media to Vevlo servers.

3. No account, advertising, tracking, or analytics

Vevlo does not create user accounts. The app does not include advertising SDKs, tracking SDKs, analytics SDKs, or a Vevlo backend for media or transcript processing. Vevlo does not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising.

This static website also does not use advertising, tracking, or analytics tools.

4. Purchases

Vevlo may offer a one-time Pro Lifetime purchase. Purchases, purchase verification, and Restore Purchase are handled by Apple through StoreKit and the App Store. Vevlo does not receive or store your payment card number or other payment-card information. Apple’s handling of purchase information is governed by Apple’s policies.

5. Sharing exports

When you use the iOS Share Sheet to send a TXT, SRT, or VTT file to another app, service, or person, that recipient’s privacy practices apply to the shared file. Vevlo does not control how another recipient handles a file after you share it.

6. Support email

If you email Vevlo Support, we use the information you voluntarily provide in your message—such as your email address, device details, app version, and description of the issue—to respond to and resolve your support request. We do not ask for passwords or payment-card information. Please avoid sending sensitive media or transcripts unless you choose to do so and it is necessary for support.

Support correspondence is retained only as long as reasonably necessary to handle the request, maintain support records, or meet legal obligations.

7. Children

Vevlo is not designed to collect personal information from children. The app does not provide social features, user profiles, or direct messaging. If you believe a child has provided information directly to us through support email, contact us at the address below.

8. Changes to this policy

If Vevlo’s data practices change, this policy will be updated before or when the relevant app update is released. Material changes will be reflected by an updated date on this page.
