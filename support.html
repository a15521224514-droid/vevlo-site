Common questions

Which file types does Vevlo support?

V1 supports MP4, MOV, MP3, M4A, and WAV files. You can choose video from Photos or import supported video and audio from Files.

Does Vevlo require an account?

No. Vevlo does not require an account or sign-in to import, transcribe, edit, or export supported media.

Where are my media and transcripts stored?

Imported media and transcript projects are stored in Vevlo’s local device storage. You can delete a project from the app when you no longer need it.

Which languages are available?

V1 supports English transcription. Review your transcript before publishing because recognition quality depends on the recording.

How do purchases work?

Vevlo offers a one-time Pro Lifetime purchase. Purchases and Restore Purchase are handled through Apple’s App Store and StoreKit.
